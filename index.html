Questions owners ask us.

What happens after my 25 free jobs?

Buy job credits from ₦1,000 inside the app. Credits do not expire. If you are busy every week, the monthly Unlimited plan may suit you better.

Does it work on my phone?

Yes. CleanCrew is built for use on your phone at the counter or on site, and also works on a computer.

Can my staff be added to jobs?

Yes. Assign staff to jobs and log what each job cost you, so you can see profit per job.

Can customers book me online?

Yes. Share your booking link and requests appear in your dashboard for you to review.

Does it work in Ghana?

Yes. CleanCrew is available in Nigeria and Ghana, and amounts show in your local currency inside the app.
